# Privacy policy

This policy explains which of your data SynaptixFit uses, what for, who it is shared with and how you can control it. We have written it in plain language. If anything differs from the Spanish version, the Spanish version prevails.

## In short

- We use your data to organise your studies and your training. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.
- How you feel each day (sleep, energy, soreness, pain) and "My cycle" are health data: they are only stored if you give a separate permission, and you can withdraw it whenever you want.
- Some features use artificial intelligence: we only send what is needed and never your name, your email or "My cycle".
- If you record a run with GPS, we use your precise location only while you record. Your route is private: only you can see it.
- You can download all your data or delete your account from the app at any time.

## Which data we use

- Your account: name, email, password (stored encrypted, nobody can read it), profile photo if you add one and, if you sign in with Google, your Google identifier.
- Your studies: university, degree, year, subjects, timetable, classes, assignments, exams, study sessions in your calendar, notes, files you upload, review questions and your answers.
- Your training: routines, sessions, sets, weights, times, activities and matches you log, running workouts, challenges, streaks, level and badges.
- Your precise location, only while you record a run with GPS (also with the screen off, with an ongoing "Recording your run" notice on the phone): the route you take, with the time and altitude of each point, to calculate distance, pace, elevation gain and per-kilometre splits and to draw the map. Location is never used when you are not recording.
- Your date of birth, if you give it (it is optional): we use it to work out your age for calories and suggestions, and to wish you a happy birthday. It never shows on your public profile, it is included in the copy of your data you download and it is deleted when you empty or delete your account.
- Your wellbeing profile, if you fill it in: sex, weight, height, activity level and, if you gave it before the date of birth existed, your age.
- Your daily check-in, only if you give your permission: how you slept, stress, energy, soreness and areas with pain.
- "My cycle", only if you turn it on: period days, energy and discomfort you log and your notes.
- Photos of paper notes: we read them to turn them into text and do not keep the photo. Only the note you decide to save is stored.
- What you share in the community: posts, comments, shared notes, friendships and visible badges.
- Reminders: your reminder preferences and, if you turn it on, the weekly summary email.
- Technical data: the IP address and the type of browser or phone that reach the server, needed for the app to work and stay secure.

## Health data

The daily check-in (sleep, stress, energy, soreness, pain) and "My cycle" are health data. That is why accepting this policy is not enough: the first time you use the daily check-in we will ask for a separate permission, and another one when you turn on "My cycle". Without it nothing is stored.

You can withdraw the permission whenever you want:

- Daily check-in: Profile › Settings › Privacy and data › How you feel data. When you withdraw it we stop using it and you can also delete the earlier ones.
- "My cycle": turn it off from its screen; you can delete everything you logged.

Cycle data is only visible to you: it is not shared with anyone, not even the artificial intelligence, and it does not appear on your public profile.

## Location and routes

- Location is only requested right before your first run, explaining what it is for. If you do not allow it, you can log your activities by hand.
- While you record, the route is first saved on your phone (so it is not lost if the app closes) and sent to our server when you finish or when there is a connection.
- Your route is always private: it does not appear on your public profile or in the community. When you share a run only the figures are shared, never the map, because it could reveal where you live.
- The map is drawn with MapTiler: your phone requests the images of the area shown (like any map). We do not send it your route, your name or your email.
- They are deleted when you delete the run or your account. They are included in the copy of your data that you download yourself, but not in the one an administrator may prepare to handle a request.

## What we use it for

- To make the app work as we promise when you sign up: plan your studies and training, track your progress, suggest weights, reviews and changes, and remind you.
- To adapt your training to how you feel, with your daily check-in and, if you use it, "My cycle". This only happens because you give the permission.
- For the community, showing what you decide to share according to the privacy you choose.
- To keep the app secure and fix bugs, with technical logs and limits against abuse. It is a reasonable thing we need to do and it does not harm you.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

## Artificial intelligence

Some features use DeepSeek, an artificial intelligence provider: generating routines, summaries and review questions, tidying the text of note photos, reading timetables or enrolments and suggesting goals. We only send what each request needs and never your name or your email. When suggesting a routine, for example, we send your age (worked out, never your date of birth), sex, weight, height, activity level, your study hours and, if you gave the daily check-in permission, how you are today (sleep, stress, areas with pain). "My cycle" data is never sent.

## Who it is shared with

Only with companies we need for the app to work, which work for us and do not use your data for anything else:

- Our server, rented from OVH in France, where the database and the app live.
- Cloudflare: protects and speeds up the connection to the app and stores the files you upload.
- DeepSeek: artificial intelligence, as explained above. Its servers may be outside the European Union, including China.
- Google: only if you sign in with your Google account, to check who you are.
- Resend: sends the password recovery and weekly summary emails.
- MapTiler: serves the images of the Running map; it receives the area of the map shown, not your route.

When one of these companies is outside the European Union, data is sent with the contracts and safeguards required by European data protection law (GDPR).

## How long we keep it

- While you have the account.
- If you delete something (a note, a session, a cycle day), it disappears from the app immediately.
- If you delete your account, we delete your data and your files. Encrypted backups renew themselves and deleted data disappears from them when they expire.
- What the app stores on your phone (drafts, scheduled reminders, the run in progress or waiting to be sent) is deleted when you sign out or uninstall it.

## Your rights

You can ask at any time to see, correct, delete or take your data with you, object to or limit a use, and withdraw your permissions. From the app:

- Download your data: Profile › Settings › Privacy and data › Export my data.
- Delete your account: Profile › Settings › Privacy and data › Delete my account.
- Also from the web: {{APP_URL}}/delete-account.
- For any other request, write to {{CORREO}}.

If you think we do not handle your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (https://www.aepd.es).

## Phone permissions

The app only asks for permissions when they are needed and explains first what for: notifications (reminders), on-time reminders (so reminders arrive at the exact minute), camera (photographing notes) and location (recording runs: only while you record, even with the screen off; never when you are not recording). You can refuse or remove them in the phone settings: the app keeps working without them.

## Minors

SynaptixFit is meant for university students. Do not use it if you are under 14.

## Changes to this policy

If we change it, we will tell you in the app and ask you to accept it again before continuing. You will see the date of the current version next to the title.
