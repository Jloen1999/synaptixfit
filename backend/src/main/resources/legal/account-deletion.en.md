# Delete your SynaptixFit account

You can delete your account and all your data whenever you want. It is permanent: it cannot be undone.

## From the phone app

- Open SynaptixFit and go to Profile.
- Tap Settings › Privacy and data › Delete my account.
- Confirm with your password (if you sign in with Google, just tick the confirmation box).

## From the web

- Sign in at {{APP_URL}}.
- Go to Profile › Settings › Privacy and data › Delete my account and confirm.

## If you can no longer sign in

Write to {{CORREO}} from your account's email asking us to delete it. We will reply as soon as possible and within one month at the latest.

## What is deleted

- Your account, your profile and your progress: level, badges, streaks and challenges.
- Your studies: subjects, timetable, assignments, notes, uploaded files and reviews.
- Your date of birth, if you gave it.
- Your workouts, activities, runs with their GPS route, daily check-in and, if you used it, "My cycle".
- What you posted in the community and your friendships.

It is removed from the database immediately. Encrypted backups rotate automatically and your data disappears from them when they expire. Before deleting it, you can download your data from Profile › Settings › Privacy and data › Export my data.
